Hooks 

    useState - states ko manage krne ke liye 

    useContext - global state ko manage krne ke liye without prop drilling 

    useReducer - complex state management ke liye (REDUX jaisa chota version)

    useEffect - side effects handle krne ke liye jaise API call,DOM manipulation,event listner

    useRef - mutable values hold krne ke liye jo re-render trigger na kre ye to access dom 

    useMemo & useCallback- optimixation ke liye or to avoid unnecessary re renders
